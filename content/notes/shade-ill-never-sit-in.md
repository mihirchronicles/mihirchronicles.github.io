I was asked by Builders Vision team (my workplace) to share what *rising* meant to me during our annual retreat. Trees have special place in my heart so I used trees as an inspiration to write a poem (not a poet) called – Shade I'll Never Sit In. 

**Verse 1: The Seed**

Started as a seed in the cold dark ground,

Roots went searching where the light can't be found.

Nobody applauds for the work down below,

But the deeper you dig, the higher you grow.

Ring after ring, every season we passed,

With every storm the timber held fast.

Didn't skip a season, didn't fake the height,

Grew an inch at a time, till we touched the light.

**Hook (Slow Down)**

Hope without action is a wish, nothing more,

So we put the seed down in the forest floor.

We're rooted, we're rising, but not for the view,

We plant shade we'll never sit in for the kids coming through.

**Verse 2: Above My Needs**

Rising was never a title or a raise,

Never a corner office or a spotlight's praise.

The hero only rises when he lets go of his need,

Turns to serve the others, and that's when he's freed.

I look at my kids and I'm playing the long game,

Thirty summers out, will these woods look the same?

Will there still be oaks where my children can climb?

Will the creek still run clear in their kids' time?

In the old growth forest, when a giant comes down,

It doesn't just vanish, it gives back to the ground.

Seedlings take root right along its spine,

That's the legacy, one life lifting the next in line.

Storm brings the nest down, twigs scattered in the breeze,

Sun comes back up, and the bird's back in the trees.

It builds for the wings that are learning to fly,

Builds the nest so the next ones can touch the sky.

**Hook (Slow down)**

Hope without action is a wish, nothing more,

So we put the seed down in the forest floor.

We're rooted, we're rising, but not for the view,

We plant shade we'll never sit in for the kids coming through.

**Verse 3: The Builders Grove**

Look around this room, you won't find a lone pine,

It's a grove full of builders with the roots intertwined.

Redwoods run shallow, but they reach out wide,

Lock in with the neighbors so the storm can't divide.

Capital is the water, but it won't climb alone,

It rises through the living wood the forest has grown.

Allocate it well and it moves the world ahead:

Healthy food, living oceans, the soil, the watershed.

Lead before the title, invest before the return,

Plant before the harvest, give before you earn.

A forest big enough will make its own rain,

Send the clouds downwind so the far fields grow again.

That's the kind of rising that I'm talking about,

Not the tallest tree standing, but the end of the drought.

The risk is not thinking big, it is not thinking big enough,

So we dream like a forest, and we build when it is tough.

**Bricky (BV Values)**

B is for Bravery - a seed splits its shell,

Pushes through the dark with no promise it'll go well.

R is for Rigor - every ring keeps the score,

Do the work right, then do a little more.

I is Inclusivity - every species gets a seat,

From the canopy above to the fungus at our feet.

C is for Curiosity - roots wander, roots roam,

Every question in the dirt brings the water back home.

K is for Kind - the oak gives shade for free,

Shelters every traveler who'll never thank the tree.

Bricky is on our back and he is our code,

Brick by brick, root by root, down the long road.

So we build it like bricks, and we grow it like trees,

That is Bricky on my shoulder, carrying all of these.

**Hook (Slow down)**

So when they ask me what it means to rise,

It is seeing past myself through my children's eyes.

It is planting the shade I will never sit in,

So my kids and yours have a forest to rest in.

Hope without action is a wish, nothing more,

So we plant, and we rise. That is what rising is for.
